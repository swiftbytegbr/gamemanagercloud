Personal Data We Collect and Process

  • User account information: We collect information you provide when creating and managing an account, such as your email address, username, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form). You may also optionally provide your name or company details (e.g. company name, VAT number, billing address) and set notification preferences. In addition, you can upload a profile picture (avatar), which we store in encoded form, and choose an account type or role (for example standard user, administrator, or application account). For security purposes, we may process information related to two-factor authentication (2FA), including your chosen 2FA method (such as none, email, or time-based one-time password (TOTP)), temporary verification codes for email-based 2FA, a TOTP seed, hashed backup/recovery codes and the time when such codes were created. We also generate and store short-lived codes or tokens for account verification (such as email verification links) and password resets. Furthermore, we may maintain per-user limits or quotas (for example, the maximum number of teams you can own) in order to enforce our service plans and protect system stability. We use this account data to create and manage your account, authenticate you, operate and secure login flows (including two-factor authentication), enforce service limits, and communicate service information and important operational updates. We also record the date and time of your last login to determine account activity and, if necessary, to identify and delete inactive accounts in accordance with our retention policies. We will only use your contact details for marketing communications (such as offers or promotional messages related to GameManagerCloud) if you have given your explicit consent, and you may withdraw this consent at any time.
  • GMC Daemon data: When you install and run the GMC Daemon on your dedicated server, the daemon registers itself as a node within GameManagerCloud. During the one-time registration process, you enter an invite code that is provided by us so the node can be linked to the correct team. As part of this process, and continuously while the node is running, the daemon transmits technical information about the host machine and any managed game servers. This includes data such as the operating system, host name, local IP addresses, hardware resources (RAM, CPU, disk capacities), daemon/software version, configuration settings, runtime status, performance metrics (e.g. CPU and memory usage), network usage, online player counts, backups and snapshots, and remote console (RCON) command history. For the node itself, we may also process the invite code used during registration, a persistent node authentication secret, the daemon version in use, the time of the last successful connection, and (where relevant) the reason why the node disconnected or shut down.

    While the GMC Daemon is running, this technical data is periodically updated so it can be displayed in real time within the GameManagerCloud interface. We process this information to enable remote management and monitoring of your game servers, provide features such as backups and rollbacks, enforce configured limits, and maintain audit logs for security and troubleshooting. We also use aggregated server statistics (for example, CPU, RAM, network usage and player counts over time) to present historical views and performance graphs within the application. Configuration templates and setting definitions used to configure nodes and servers are stored as technical metadata and generally do not contain personal data.
  • Usage and log data: We automatically collect certain data when you use our website or API. This includes information like your IP address, browser type and version or other user agent details, referring pages, pages or endpoints you access, the dates/times of access, and similar technical information. In addition, our website (which runs on Joomla with YOOtheme) may collect standard web analytics and interaction data, such as device type, screen resolution, session duration, entry and exit pages, clicks, form submissions, internal search terms, scroll depth, and error or performance diagnostics (for example, page load times or client-side script errors). Cookies or identifiers may be used to distinguish sessions or measure aggregated visitor statistics, but we do not create profiles for advertising or cross-site tracking. We use this data to ensure the functionality of our services, maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, debug issues, monitor availability, and analyze usage trends to improve our services.
  • Sessions and security: When you log in, we generate a session identifier and record metadata such as session creation time, last accessed time, your IP address and browser user agent for that session, and the session’s expiration time. We use this information to manage and authenticate your sessions (for example, to keep you logged in, detect concurrent logins, or allow you to terminate sessions), and to help detect unauthorized access or misuse of your account.

    Our website backend (Joomla with YOOtheme) may also maintain additional administrative and security logs. These logs can include information such as administrator or editor user IDs, IP addresses, timestamps of logins or login attempts, content or configuration changes, module installations/uninstallations, and similar system events. This data is processed exclusively for the purposes of maintaining security, auditing administrative actions, and troubleshooting technical issues. Such logs are retained only as long as necessary for these purposes and are then deleted or anonymized.
  • Team and action logs: If you create or join a team on GameManagerCloud, we process information about the team and its members. This includes team profile details (such as team name and icon), team membership assignments and permissions, any pending invitations (invitee user ID, invite timestamp, and a team invite code that can be used to join the team), as well as team-level limits or quotas (for example the maximum number of nodes, servers, backups, remote console commands, action log entries, stored statistics, or team members). Teams can also use application integrations; for this purpose, we allow the creation of dedicated “application” user accounts with an identifier and a hashed secret, which are then linked to teams and granted specific permissions. We maintain an action log for teams, recording events such as creation, modification or deletion of servers or nodes, configuration changes, membership changes, and other operational actions. These logs typically note the action performed, when it happened, who (which user or application account) initiated it, which team/server/node or other entity was affected, and structured details or free-text descriptions provided for that action. We use this data to facilitate collaboration, provide transparency to team members, and support security audits and troubleshooting.
  • Communications and support (Email and YouTrack Helpdesk): If you communicate with us via email, our contact form, or the bug report feature in GameManagerCloud, we will collect and store the content of your communications along with your contact details (such as your email address and name). Support tickets and bug reports are processed in our YouTrack Helpdesk system. Depending on how the ticket is created, the following additional data may be collected and stored:
    • Your email address and any associated mail server metadata
    • The full content of your message and any attachments or details you provide
    • If the ticket is generated by the GMC backend: your user ID, email, name, team ID, team owner’s name and email, and (optionally) your Discord username
    • The subject line and message body of the ticket or bug report
    We retain these communications and support records only as long as necessary to respond to you, resolve your request, improve our support processes, and maintain an appropriate audit trail. After this period, tickets are deleted or anonymized unless legal obligations require longer retention.
  • Billing and payment information: If you purchase a subscription or other paid services (for example, if we introduce paid plans in the future), we will collect information necessary for billing and payment processing. This may include your full name, billing address, email, company name (if applicable), and VAT ID or other tax identification (if applicable). Payment transactions are handled by our third-party payment partner (Paddle, see below). GameManagerCloud is a service intended for use by adults. We do not knowingly allow children under the age of 16 to register an account. If you are under 16, you should not use our service. If we become aware of personal data collected from a child under 16 without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies to recognize you and operate the service. Specifically, when you log in to GameManagerCloud, we use a session cookie to keep you authenticated; this cookie contains a session ID and typically expires when you log out or close your browser. We do not set any cookies that remain indefinitely. If you choose a "remember me" or similar option at login, a persistent authentication cookie will be stored on your device, but it will expire automatically after about 30 days. We do not use any non-essential tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; however, core features (such as logging into your account) will not function properly without them.

Use of Cloudflare (CDN and Security)

We use Cloudflare, Inc. as a content delivery network (CDN) and security provider for our services. This means that traffic to and from our website and API is routed through Cloudflare’s global network to improve performance and provide protection against malicious attacks (for example, DDoS attacks or abusive bots). In the process of providing these services, Cloudflare may process certain data that could be personal data, such as the IP address of your device, your DNS queries, and information from your web browser’s requests (including URLs and headers).

Cloudflare may also set the following security-related cookies on your device, which are considered strictly necessary for the security and functioning of the service:

  • __cf_bm – used to distinguish between humans and bots and to protect the site from automated abuse. This cookie is temporary and usually expires after about 30 minutes.
  • cf_clearance – used to store proof that a user has passed a security challenge (e.g. CAPTCHA). This allows Cloudflare to grant continued access without repeated challenges. The expiration period depends on the challenge and site configuration, typically from a few hours up to one day.
  • __cfruid – used for rate limiting and to manage traffic for reliable service delivery. This cookie is session-based and expires when you close your browser.

These cookies are strictly functional and cannot be disabled without impacting access to the service. They do not track you across websites and are only used for security and performance purposes.

Cloudflare acts as our data processor under the GDPR, processing personal data solely for the purposes we determine (site performance and security). A Data Processing Agreement with Cloudflare is in place to ensure GDPR compliance. Cloudflare may process data in the United States or other countries outside the EU/EEA. When it transfers personal data out of Europe, Cloudflare uses appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. (Cloudflare has also certified its compliance with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as well as the equivalent frameworks for the UK and Switzerland.) Our use of Cloudflare is based on our legitimate interests in delivering a fast and secure service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). For more information, please refer to Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Your Data Protection Rights

As a data subject, you have a number of rights regarding your personal data. These rights are granted by the GDPR for users in the European Union (and similar rights are extended to users in other jurisdictions, as described below).   • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct or update any personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete. If you find that any information associated with your account is incorrect (for example, a misspelled name or an outdated address), you can ask us to fix it.   • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances, often referred to as the “right to be forgotten.” You can request that we erase your data if it is no longer necessary for us to retain it for the purposes it was collected, or if you have withdrawn consent (where consent was the legal basis) or successfully objected to a processing activity, or if we processed your data unlawfully, or if erasure is required to comply with a legal obligation. Please note that this right is not absolute – sometimes we may need to retain certain information (e.g. for legal compliance or legitimate interests as described above). If that is the case, we will inform you of the reasons we cannot delete the data immediately and will delete it as soon as those reasons no longer apply.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we limit the processing of your personal data under certain conditions. This means we would store your data but temporarily refrain from using it or sharing it until the restriction is lifted. You might exercise this right if, for example, you contest the accuracy of your data (you can ask us to stop processing until we verify or correct it), or if you object to our processing based on legitimate interests (while we consider your objection request), or if you need us to keep data that we would otherwise delete because you need it for a legal claim. When processing is restricted, we will mark the affected data and process it only for specific reasons (such as storing it, or as needed for legal claims, or with your consent, etc.).
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive the personal data that you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to have that data transmitted to another controller where technically feasible. This right applies when the processing is based on your consent or on a contract and is carried out by automated means. In practice, if you request it, we will provide you with an export of your account data and any other personal data that you have actively provided to GameManagerCloud (and that we still have), in a format like JSON or CSV, so that you can import it into another service if you wish.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data at any time if the processing is based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or on the performance of a task in the public interest (which we do not engage in). If you object, we will evaluate your request and will stop or modify the processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds to continue that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or unless the processing is needed for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. Where your personal data is processed for direct marketing purposes, you have the right to object at any time and we will cease such marketing immediately. For example, if we ever send you a marketing email based on our legitimate interest and you opt-out or object, we will honor that and not send further marketing messages.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If we are processing any of your personal data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before the withdrawal. If you withdraw consent, we will stop the processing that was based on it. For instance, if you gave consent for a newsletter, you can withdraw it by unsubscribing, and we will not send you further newsletters. There is no penalty for withdrawing consent; however, note that if a certain feature of our service relies on your consent to process personal data, we may not be able to provide that feature to you after you withdraw consent.
  • Right to Complain: If you believe we have processed your personal data in a manner that is unlawful or violates your rights, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the EU, you can contact the data protection authority in the country of your residence, place of work, or where the issue occurred. For example, in Germany you can contact the data protection authority of the relevant federal state (our company is based in Hesse, so the Hessian data protection commissioner is an option), and in the Netherlands you could contact the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, etc. If you are in the EEA, you can find a list of supervisory authorities on the European Data Protection Board website. Similarly, UK residents can reach out to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), and Swiss residents can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concerns directly before you approach an authority, so please feel free to contact us with any issues.

These rights apply to personal data that we control, including data that may be held by our service providers on our behalf. For example, if you request an export or deletion, we will also retrieve and include relevant data from our subprocessors (Cloudflare, Paddle, etc.) as needed to fully comply with your request.
